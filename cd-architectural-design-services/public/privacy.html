YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

This notice explains how information submitted through the C + D Architectural Design Services website is intended to be used when you inquire about a project.

Owner review before launch: confirm the studio's legal/business identity, public contact channels, data-retention period, and live hosting configuration before publishing this notice as final.

Information you provide

The project brief may collect your name, email address, mobile number, preferred contact method, project location, approximate site measurements, room requirements, preferred design direction, budget range, timeline, notes, and files you choose to upload.

Why it is used

The information is intended to help the studio understand your inquiry, assess the requested scope, review reference files, and contact you about questions or next steps. Submitting a brief is not a project agreement.

Where submissions go

During local development, test submissions are saved on the computer running the website and are not sent to the studio. On a configured live deployment, inquiry details are intended to be stored in the project's private database, while uploaded references are intended to be held in private file storage. If email notifications are configured, a summary of the inquiry and its attachments may also be sent to the designated studio inbox.

The studio should verify the active storage, email, access-control, and backup settings before accepting real client information.

Files and sensitive information

Only upload images and documents you have permission to share, such as inspiration images, sketches, site photos, or plans. Do not upload identification documents, payment details, passwords, or unrelated sensitive records.

Access, retention, and requests

Access should be limited to people who need the information to handle the inquiry and service providers used to operate the website. The studio must confirm a retention period and secure deletion procedure for inquiry data, email copies, and uploaded files. You may use the verified business contact channels on the home page to ask about your information or request a correction or deletion, subject to applicable obligations.

Contact

Use the available channels on the contact section or send a project inquiry. The studio should add and verify its business contact details before this website is launched publicly.

Notice for C + D Architectural Design Services. Last reviewed: October 2026. This is a practical website draft, not legal advice.
