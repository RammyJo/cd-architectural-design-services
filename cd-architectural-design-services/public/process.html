A HELPFUL START

What to prepare if you can.

You can begin with incomplete information. The goal is to share enough context for a productive first discussion.

01

Property basics

Project location, approximate lot dimensions, any existing structures, and what you know about access or site conditions.

02

How the space should work

People who will use it, rooms you need, privacy needs, everyday routines, and must-have features.

03

Inspiration and priorities

Reference images, sketches, preferred atmosphere, rough budget, and a target timeline if you have one.
